An original sun-soaked coastal open-world crime game. You are Rook Marlowe, a getaway driver who owes a debt to the fixer Mama Lou — and the Kings crew stole her shipment. Four story missions pay the debt off, one job at a time.

Drive and walk a living city: traffic cars looping the streets, pedestrians on the sidewalks, a 0–5 star wanted system with black-and-white police cruisers in pursuit, hitscan combat, a HUD with health, money, wanted stars and a live minimap, synthesized WebAudio sound (engine hum, sirens, gunshots), and localStorage saves. All names, story and art are original — nothing from Rockstar. Static files, zero network at runtime.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080

## Controls

WASD drive / walk · Space enter or steal a car · Mouse click or F shoot · P save · M mute

## Screenshots

The four staged shots are defined in `shots.json` (`?shot=start|drive|chase|mission`, each with `&seed=1`) and rendered by `game/src/shots.js`.
